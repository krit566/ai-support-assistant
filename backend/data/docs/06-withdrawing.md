# Withdrawing an application

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## When you can withdraw
You can withdraw an application while it is Applied or Shortlisted, from the My Applications page. Once an interview is scheduled you cannot withdraw through the portal.

## Withdrawing after an interview is scheduled
Write to the Placement Cell at least 24 hours before the interview with a valid reason. Not attending a scheduled interview without informing the Placement Cell counts as a no-show.

## No-show penalty
Two no-shows in an academic year suspend your portal access for 30 days.
