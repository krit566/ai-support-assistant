# Documents and resume upload

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Resume format
Upload your resume as a single PDF of at most 2 MB, preferably one page. The file name should be RollNumber_Name.pdf.

## Mandatory documents
Upload your Class X and Class XII marksheets and your latest semester grade card before applying to any job. Missing documents block applications.

## Changing your resume
You may upload a new resume at any time. Each application keeps the resume version that was current when you applied.
