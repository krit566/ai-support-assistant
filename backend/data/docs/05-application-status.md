# What each application status means

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Statuses
- Applied: your application has been received.
- Shortlisted: the company selected you for the next round.
- Interview: you have an interview scheduled; details are in the Interviews tab.
- Offered: you received an offer.
- Rejected: the company did not take your application forward.
- Withdrawn: you withdrew the application.

## Changing status
Statuses only move forward. Rejected, Offered and Withdrawn are final.
