# Registering on the placement portal

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Who can register
All final-year and pre-final-year B.Tech and MCA students can register. Registration opens in the first week of July every year and closes on 31 July.

## How to register
Sign in with your university email address (ending in @geu.example.edu) and complete your profile: roll number, branch, current CGPA, graduation year and skills. You cannot change your roll number or branch after registration; contact the Placement Cell if they are wrong.

## Registration fee
There is no registration fee. Any message asking you to pay for placement registration is fraudulent and should be reported to the Placement Cell.
