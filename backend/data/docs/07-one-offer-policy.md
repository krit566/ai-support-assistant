# One-offer policy and dream companies

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## The rule
Once you accept an offer, you are placed and cannot apply to further companies, except dream companies.

## Dream companies
A dream company is one offering a CTC at least 1.5 times your current accepted offer. A placed student may apply to dream companies; if selected, the earlier offer is released back to the company.

## Declining an offer
If you decline an offer after accepting it, you are barred from further placement drives for the rest of the academic year.
