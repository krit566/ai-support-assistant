# Offer letters and joining

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## When you get the offer letter
Companies send offer letters directly or through the Placement Cell, usually within 4 to 8 weeks of the result.

## Accepting
Accept the offer on the portal within 7 days of receiving it, otherwise it is marked as declined.

## Joining date
The joining date is decided by the company. The Placement Cell cannot change it, but can forward a written request.
