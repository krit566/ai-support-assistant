# Your data and privacy

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## What is shared with companies
Only the data needed for a job is shared: name, roll number, branch, CGPA, resume and contact email. Phone numbers are shared only after you are shortlisted.

## Deleting your data
Profiles are archived one year after graduation and deleted after three years. You can request earlier deletion through the grievance form.
