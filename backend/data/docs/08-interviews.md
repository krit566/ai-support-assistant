# Interview schedule and conduct

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Scheduling
Interview slots are published in the Interviews tab at least 48 hours in advance. Slots cannot be swapped between students without written approval from the Placement Cell.

## Online interviews
For online interviews, join the meeting link 10 minutes early, keep your camera on and use a quiet room. Do not record the interview.

## Dress code
Formal or business-casual attire is expected for both online and in-person interviews.
