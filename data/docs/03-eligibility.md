# Eligibility rules for jobs

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Branch and CGPA
Each job lists eligible branches and a minimum CGPA. The portal only shows the Apply button if your branch is eligible and your CGPA is equal to or above the minimum. The cut-off is applied exactly: a CGPA of 6.99 does not meet a 7.0 cut-off.

## Backlogs
Students with an active backlog (an uncleared subject) are not eligible for most companies. The job post states whether active backlogs are allowed. Cleared backlogs do not count against you.

## Gap years
A gap of up to one year in education is accepted by most companies; the job post mentions any stricter rule.
