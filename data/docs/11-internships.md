# Internships, PPOs and full-time offers

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Summer internships
Pre-final-year students can apply to summer internships through the same portal. Internships typically run 8 to 12 weeks in May to July.

## Pre-placement offers
A pre-placement offer (PPO) is a full-time offer made by the internship company. A PPO counts as a placement under the one-offer policy once you accept it.

## Internship and placement together
Accepting a summer internship does not stop you from sitting for full-time placements.
