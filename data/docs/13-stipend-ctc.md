# Stipend and CTC information

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## What CTC means
CTC (cost to company) is the total annual cost of employing you, including fixed pay, bonuses and benefits. Your in-hand salary is lower than the CTC.

## Where to find it
The CTC or stipend for each job is shown on the job post. If a company changes the package after the offer, the Placement Cell informs all selected students by email.
