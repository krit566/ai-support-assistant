# Pre-placement talks and attendance

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Pre-placement talks
Companies often hold a pre-placement talk (PPT) before the test. Attendance at the PPT is mandatory for all students who applied to that company.

## Missing a PPT
If you miss the PPT without prior permission, your application to that company is cancelled.

## Permission
Request permission through the portal at least one day in advance, giving the reason (for example a university exam clash).
