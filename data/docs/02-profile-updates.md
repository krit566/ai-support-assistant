# Updating your profile and CGPA

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## What you can edit
You can edit your name, skills, phone number and resume at any time from the Profile page.

## CGPA updates
Your CGPA is updated by the Placement Cell from the official results after every semester, usually within 10 working days of results being declared. You cannot edit CGPA yourself. If your CGPA on the portal does not match your marksheet, raise a correction request with a scanned copy of the marksheet.

## Verification
Profiles are verified by your department coordinator. An unverified profile cannot apply to any job.
