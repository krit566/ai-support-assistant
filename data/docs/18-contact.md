# Contacting the Placement Cell

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Office
The Placement Cell office is on the ground floor of the Administrative Block, open Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM.

## Email
Write to placements@geu.example.edu from your university email address. Messages from personal email addresses are not answered, to protect student data.
