# Raising a grievance

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## How to raise one
Use the Help, then Grievance form on the portal. Describe the issue, attach evidence and choose a category (portal problem, company conduct, eligibility, other).

## Response time
The Placement Cell acknowledges grievances within 2 working days and aims to resolve them within 10 working days.

## Escalation
If a grievance is not resolved in 10 working days, it can be escalated to the Dean of Student Welfare.
