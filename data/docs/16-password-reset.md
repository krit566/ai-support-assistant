# Login problems and password reset

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Forgot password
Use Forgot password on the login page. A reset link valid for 30 minutes is sent to your university email.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or reset your password.

## University email not working
Contact the university IT helpdesk; the Placement Cell cannot reset your university email account.
