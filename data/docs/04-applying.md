# Applying to a job

> Fictional help-centre article for a demo project (not a real university policy).

## Steps
Open the job from the Jobs page, read the job description and eligibility, then press Apply. You can apply only once to each job.

## Deadlines
Applications close at 11:59 PM on the deadline date shown on the job post. Late applications are not accepted, and the Placement Cell cannot reopen an application window for individual students.

## After applying
Your application starts in the Applied state. You will get an email and a portal notification whenever its status changes.
